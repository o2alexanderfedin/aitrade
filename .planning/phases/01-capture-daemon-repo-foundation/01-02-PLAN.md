Irreplaceable Binance USD-M perpetual-futures L1 + trade history is being recorded reliably from
day 1 — this plan is the point where that recording actually starts continuously, not as a
one-shot proof.

<objective>
Turn Plan 01's one-shot live proof into a continuously-running, restart-safe, durable
single-connection daemon: raw verbatim archive, atomic rotated Parquet, seq that survives a
restart, a loud startup failure on a silent wrong-class subscription, graceful shutdown, and
`caffeinate`-wrapped sleep prevention. This is the plan where capture uptime — the
schedule-critical deliverable — actually starts accruing. Redundancy (second connection) and
dedup are explicitly NOT in scope here (Plan 03) — this is a single connection, hardened for
continuous unattended operation, not yet redundant.

Purpose: every day this daemon is not running is a day of permanently lost L1 history. Plan 01
proved the stack works; this plan makes it run.
Output: `mvp/data/capture/{rotation,ws_client,daemon}.py`, `mvp/configs/capture.toml`, and a
running daemon process on this Mac writing to `/Volumes/ProjectsSSD/...`, confirmed by a human.
</objective>
