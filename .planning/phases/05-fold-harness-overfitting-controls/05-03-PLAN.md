<objective>
Close the loop between budget and segment issuance (D-05-14) and ship the negative-result log
(D-05-22, EVAL-04). This is the plan that makes P1's budget.py and P2's segments.py talk to each
other: exhaustion becomes a real, named refusal, and a new manifest cannot silently reuse an
exhausted validation window.

Purpose: D-05-14 is explicit that the "fresh window" requirement is enforced at TWO points --
the accessor's own refusal when a look is attempted against an exhausted segment (P1 already has
this for free, since `budget.record_look` will raise once this plan adds the allowance comparison),
and manifest ISSUANCE refusing a new overlapping validation window before it is even written. This
plan builds the second half and the negative-result log alongside it, since both are MLflow-first
constructs sharing the same query shape.
Output: `harness/budget.py` gains exhaustion; `harness/segments.py` gains the issuance-time overlap
refusal; `harness/negative_log.py` and its CLI are new.
</objective>
