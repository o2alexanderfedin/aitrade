<objective>
Turn decision rows into supervised targets: the primary `ret_10s_mid` plus the three diagnostic horizons, computed by a backward as-of rule that is explicit about the three ways a label can fail to exist -- past the end of the data, across a capture gap, and before the first quote of the partition.

Purpose: FEAT-04, and the half of the leakage story that features alone cannot carry. A label is the ONLY thing in this pipeline that is allowed to look forward, so exactly how far forward it looks has to be a declared, measured number rather than an emergent property of a join. `mid_{t+h}` names a row that does not exist -- no quote lands exactly at `t + 10s` -- and the two available conventions disagree on 0.26% of rows. That is small, and it is exactly the kind of unrecorded choice that makes two runs irreproducible.
Output: `features/labels.py`, the label catalogue pinned to the chosen convention, and the measured quantization recorded where Phase 5 and Phase 8 will trip over it.
</objective>

- `big_quote_gaps(quote_etime: np.ndarray, threshold_ns: int) -> tuple[np.ndarray, np.ndarray]` -- the `(start, end)` etimes of every consecutive-quote interval longer than `threshold_ns`. - `compute_labels(decision_etime, decision_mid, quote_etime, quote_mid, *, horizons_ns, gap_threshold_ns) -> tuple[dict[str, np.ndarray], dict]` -- for each horizon, `idx = np.searchsorted(quote_etime, decision_etime + h, side="right") - 1` (this IS the prevailing/backward-as-of rule; `side="left"` would be the other convention, and the difference is 0.26% of rows). ## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| decision row t -> the future | The label is the one value permitted to look forward; how far is a declared number, not an emergent one |
| day D partition -> day D+1 curated data | The label tail reads a neighbouring day, crossing a partition boundary |
| capture gap ledger -> build inputs | The operational ledger is read-only to agents; letting it in would make the lake non-self-sufficient |
